**What it shows:**
This boxplot compares customer age distributions across churn categories ("Negative" for non-churned, "Positive" for churned). The vertical axis measures age in years ranging from 18 to 65+.

**Key finding:**
Age distributions between churned and retained customers are nearly identical, with churned customers having a slightly higher median age (~44 years vs. ~41 years) and heavily overlapping interquartile ranges (31–55 vs. 29–53 years).

**Business implication:**
Demographic age is not a strong standalone predictor of churn, meaning marketing and retention teams should avoid prioritizing age-based segmentation for churn prevention campaigns.

**Confidence caveat:**
Univariate boxplots ignore potential interaction effects where age might influence churn when combined with contract type, internet service, or payment method.

**Suggestion:** Shift focus from age targeting to actionable operational levers such as contract type, monthly charges, and onboarding support for high-risk internet service plans.
